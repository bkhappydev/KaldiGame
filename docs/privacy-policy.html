Kaldi Game – Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026

Who we are

Kaldi Game (“the app”, package com.aynu.kids.kaldi_app) is made by Aynu Labs PLC. This policy explains how the app handles information. Questions: aynulabs@gmail.com.

Made for children

Kaldi is a learning game for young children. We designed it to follow the Google Play Families Policy and children’s privacy laws such as COPPA and GDPR-K. Children can play without an account and without typing any personal details.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information: no names, emails, phone numbers, photos, contacts, location or device/advertising identifiers. The app does not need an internet connection to play.

What stays on the device

To remember progress, the app saves the stars earned in each quiz, the rewards won, the chosen language and music setting, and the accessibility choices (audio quiz, read aloud, voice speed and volume) on the device itself. This never leaves the device, and is deleted when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared.

Read aloud and audio quiz

When read aloud or the audio quiz is turned on, the app speaks using the text-to-speech engine built into the phone. The app itself sends nothing anywhere and does not record or listen to the child.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

The app shows no third-party advertising, contains no analytics or tracking tools, and has no in-app purchases or chat.

App updates

When the app starts, it asks the Google Play Store app on the phone whether a newer version of Kaldi is available and, if so, shows Google Play’s update screen. The app sends no information about the child for this.

Links that leave the app

A few buttons open other apps: the promotion cards and Share open Google Play or the device’s share menu, and Contact Us opens the email app, Telegram (t.me/aynukidssupport) or the phone dialer (0969765009). Those apps have their own privacy policies. We do not receive any information when they are used, unless you choose to message or call us.

If you contact us

If a parent contacts us by email (aynulabs@gmail.com), on Telegram or by phone (0969765009), we use the contact details and message only to reply, and delete them when no longer needed. Please do not send us a child’s personal information.

Parents’ rights

Because we hold no data about your child, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To erase the progress saved on the device, uninstall the app or clear its data in the device settings.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date, and publish it in the app and on our Google Play page before the change takes effect.
